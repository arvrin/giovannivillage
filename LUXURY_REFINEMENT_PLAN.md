# Giovanni Village - Ultra-Luxury Design Refinement Plan

## Design Philosophy: Aman-Level Sophistication

**Benchmark Properties:**
- Aman Resorts (minimal, zen, editorial photography)
- Six Senses (natural luxury, generous spacing, earth tones)
- Oberoi Hotels (refined elegance, sophisticated typography)
- The Leela Palace (regal yet understated)

---

## Current Issues & Refinements Needed

### 1. **TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY** (Critical)
**Current Issues:**
- Headings not bold/impactful enough
- Body text sizes inconsistent
- Letter spacing not applied correctly
- Line heights too tight in places

**Refinements:**
- ✅ Hero titles: 96px desktop (currently likely smaller)
- ✅ Generous line-height: 1.7-1.8 for all body text
- ✅ Letter-spacing: 2px for ALL uppercase text
- ✅ Heading font weight: Always 600-700 (semibold-bold)
- ✅ Serif headings (Playfair Display) vs Sans body (Manrope)

---

### 2. **SPACING & BREATHABILITY** (Critical)
**Current Issues:**
- Sections too cramped together
- Content feels cluttered
- Not enough white space around elements

**Refinements:**
- ✅ Section padding: Minimum 120px (7.5rem) between major sections
- ✅ Component padding: 48-72px (3-4.5rem) internal spacing
- ✅ Container max-width: 1440px with 120px side padding on desktop
- ✅ Generous margins: Never less than 24px between related elements
- ✅ Hero sections: 240px top/bottom padding minimum

---

### 3. **COLOR APPLICATION** (High Priority)
**Current Issues:**
- Gold/bronze overused (should be 2% accent only)
- Not enough neutral tones (90% should be ivory/champagne)
- Backgrounds not layered correctly

**Refinements:**
- ✅ 90% neutral: Ivory (#FDFBF7) + Champagne (#E8DCC4)
- ✅ 8% text: Charcoal (#2A2826) + Bronze (#8B7355)
- ✅ 2% accent: Gold (#C9A961) - CTA buttons ONLY
- ✅ Remove gold from hover states, borders, decorative elements
- ✅ Use subtle charcoal overlays (60% opacity) on hero images

---

### 4. **IMAGERY & VISUAL HIERARCHY** (High Priority)
**Current Issues:**
- Images need better aspect ratios
- Overlays too heavy or missing
- Not enough cinematic full-bleed images

**Refinements:**
- ✅ Hero images: 70vh minimum height, full-bleed
- ✅ Image aspect ratios: 16:9 for landscapes, 3:4 for portraits
- ✅ Subtle gradient overlays: Black/60% bottom-up on heroes
- ✅ Hover effects: Gentle 1.05 scale over 1.4 seconds
- ✅ Image quality: Always high-res, optimized but not compressed

---

### 5. **HOMEPAGE REFINEMENTS** (Critical)
**Specific Issues:**
- Hero tagline placement awkward
- About section layout not premium enough
- Rooms section asymmetry needs enhancement
- CTA buttons too prominent (tone down)

**Refinements:**
- ✅ Hero: Center-aligned, minimal text, focus on imagery
- ✅ About: Two-column layout with generous spacing
- ✅ Rooms: Featured room 60/40 split, supporting rooms grid
- ✅ Experiences: Full-width cards with image backgrounds
- ✅ Testimonials: Centered quotes, large pullquote typography
- ✅ CTAs: Outline style primary, filled style sparingly

---

### 6. **NAVIGATION & HEADER** (Medium Priority)
**Current Issues:**
- Logo sizing may be off
- Navigation items spacing
- Mobile menu transition

**Refinements:**
- ✅ Logo: 56-64px height desktop, 48px mobile
- ✅ Nav items: 12px gap minimum, uppercase, 1.5px letter-spacing
- ✅ Sticky header: Smooth background transition on scroll
- ✅ Mobile menu: Full-screen overlay, large typography (40-48px)

## Key Design Principles to Follow

### **LUXURY = RESTRAINT**
1. **90% Neutral** - Use ivory and champagne for 90% of design
2. **Generous Space** - Empty space is luxury (120px between sections minimum)
3. **Slow Transitions** - Everything moves slowly (0.8-1.4s animations)
4. **Large Typography** - Hero at 96px, body at 18px minimum
5. **Minimal Borders** - Use shadows and spacing, not lines
6. **Subtle Accents** - Gold only for primary CTAs (2% of design)

### **EDITORIAL EXCELLENCE**
1. **Cinematic Images** - Full-bleed heroes, generous aspect ratios
2. **Typography Contrast** - Serif headings vs sans body
3. **Vertical Rhythm** - Consistent spacing scale (12, 24, 48, 72, 120px)
4. **Content Hierarchy** - Clear visual weight (headings bold, body regular)
5. **Pull Quotes** - 32px italic testimonials, centered

### **SOPHISTICATION MARKERS**
1. **No pure black/white** - Charcoal (#2A2826) and Ivory (#FDFBF7)
2. **Muted accents** - Never vibrant, always refined tones
3. **Consistent UI** - Same button style, same card treatment everywhere
4. **Quality imagery** - Real Giovanni photos, high-res, natural colors
5. **Tasteful animations** - Subtle, slow, sophisticated easing
