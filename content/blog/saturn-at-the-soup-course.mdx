There is a reason we put the telescope on the roof and not in a brochure. You have to look up to understand it.

Pihu sits on the rooftop, and on weekend evenings it does something most restaurants can’t: it rolls out a working telescope between courses. Saturn arrives somewhere around the soup. The Pleiades show up by dessert. In between, a tasting menu of international and local plates, candle-lit, with live music drifting over from the corner.

## Why it works here

Giovanni Village sits twenty minutes from Bhopal but a long way from its light. The sky over the estate has never really met a city — which is the whole point. On a clear night the band of the Milky Way is visible to the naked eye, and the telescope just brings the rest closer.

> Dinner under a sky like this is less a meal than a slow reminder of scale.

## Planning it

Telescope nights run on weekend evenings and are best pre-booked — the rooftop is intimate by design, and clear skies are worth reserving for. Tell the concierge your dates and we’ll line up a table, the menu, and (weather permitting) a sky worth the climb.
