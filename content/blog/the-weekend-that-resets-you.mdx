You don’t need a reason to come for the weekend. That, in a way, is the reason.

Giovanni Village is twenty minutes from Bhopal and a long way from the calendar. The suites face green. Several open onto private, open-to-sky plunge pools — temperate water under the canopy by day, under the stars by night. You sink in after a long afternoon, look up, and the night-jar calls back through the leaves.

## A day with no shape

Breakfast comes to the lawn — eggs from our hens, hand-pulled coffee, the smell of warm cardamom drifting from the kitchen. After that the day finds its own shape. A walk through the mango groves. A long lunch under the canopy. An hour at Elysium that takes its time. Nothing is scheduled too tightly; the point is to let the hours go soft.

> A short drive from the city, a long way from the week you just had.

## Built for whoever you bring

The rooms have space for the family, the quiet for a couple, and the calm for someone who simply needs to stop. Tell the concierge how many of you and what you’re after, and we’ll match you to the right room — and leave the rest of the weekend deliberately empty.
