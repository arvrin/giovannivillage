Bhopal rewards a slow weekend. It is a city of lakes and old quarters with a tiger reserve on its doorstep and a UNESCO World Heritage Site an hour away — enough for two unhurried days without ever feeling rushed. Here is how we'd spend a weekend in and around Bhopal.

## Day one: the city of lakes

Bhopal is built around water, and the best way to begin is on it.

- **Upper Lake (Bada Talab):** the city's great lake — a sunrise walk or a boat ride along the Boat Club end is the gentlest possible start.
- **The old city:** lose an hour in the lanes around Chowk and Sadar Manzil — the markets, the mosques, the everyday texture of old Bhopal.
- **State museums and galleries:** the Tribal Museum and the Museum of Man (IGRMS) are among the finest in the country and easy to underestimate.

Eat where Bhopal eats, then retreat. If you'd rather wake up to forest than traffic, base your weekend at [Giovanni Village](/about), on the edge of Ratapani, and treat the city as a day trip.

## Day two: rock art and forest

The second day is the one people remember.

### Bhimbetka — UNESCO rock shelters

About an hour from the city, the **Bhimbetka rock shelters** hold paintings tens of thousands of years old, sheltered in dramatic sandstone formations. It is one of the oldest records of human life on the subcontinent, and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Go early, before the heat.

### Bhojpur

Close by stands the **Bhojeshwar Temple at Bhojpur**, with its colossal, unfinished Shiva lingam — a single, astonishing room of a temple that never received its roof.

### A Ratapani safari

End the weekend in the forest. [Ratapani Tiger Reserve](/blog/ratapani-tiger-reserve-guide) is minutes from the resort, and an afternoon or early-morning safari is the kind of thing you book on a whim and remember for a year. We arrange permits, a jeep and a naturalist through the [travel desk](/contact).

> The best version of a Bhopal weekend has a lake at one end and a forest at the other.
