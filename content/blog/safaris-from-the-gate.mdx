Most wildlife lodges are an hour from their forest. Ours is five minutes from the gate.

Ratapani Tiger Reserve runs to roughly five hundred square kilometres of teak and bamboo — leopard, sloth bear, more than two hundred bird species, and one of the healthiest naturally breeding tiger populations in the country. From the rooms, the boundary is a short drive away, which changes the shape of a safari morning entirely.

## The morning

A naturalist meets you before the world wakes. Pre-booked jeeps leave for the early slot — the cool, low-lit hours when the forest is busiest — and you are back in time for a farm breakfast. There is an afternoon slot too, for those who would rather sleep in.

> The reserve is quietest at the edges of the day. That is when it shows you the most.

## Booking it

Ratapani requires pre-booking, and the slots are limited, so we arrange it through the travel desk when you reserve your room. Tell us your dates and we will hold a jeep and a naturalist for the morning you choose.

You do not have to be a wildlife person to go. Plenty of guests book it on a whim and come back changed — not because they saw a tiger (you might, you might not), but because of the hour, the light, and the particular silence of a forest that has not yet noticed you.
