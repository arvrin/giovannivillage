Giovanni Village sits on the southern edge of Bhopal, minutes from the gate of [Ratapani Tiger Reserve](/blog/ratapani-tiger-reserve-guide). Close enough to the city to arrive easily, far enough that the only sound is the forest. Here is how to reach us, whichever way you're travelling.

## By air

The nearest airport is **Raja Bhoj Airport (BHO)** in Bhopal, connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Indore and other major cities. From the airport, the estate is a comfortable drive across the city to its southern edge. Tell us your flight details and we can arrange a transfer through the [travel desk](/contact).

## By train

Bhopal is a major junction on the central railway line, with two main stations:

- **Bhopal Junction (BPL)**
- **Rani Kamlapati / Habibganj (RKMP)** — convenient for trains from Delhi and the south.

From either station, the estate is a short drive away.

## By road

Bhopal is well linked by highway to Indore, Nagpur and the wider state, which makes Giovanni Village an easy [weekend getaway](/blog/weekend-getaways-from-bhopal). The estate lies toward the Ratapani side of the city, so you trade city traffic for forest road as you arrive.

> The last few minutes of the drive are the best part. The city falls away, and the forest begins.

## Arriving for a safari

If a [Ratapani safari](/blog/safaris-from-the-gate) is the reason for your trip, timing your arrival matters. The early morning slot is the strongest, so we recommend arriving the evening before — settle into a [room](/rooms), sleep early, and let the naturalist meet you before the world wakes. We arrange permits, jeeps and naturalists in advance, so the only thing left for you is to show up.
