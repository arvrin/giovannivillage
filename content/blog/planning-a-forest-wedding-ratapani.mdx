A forest wedding is not a hall wedding moved outdoors. It is a different kind of day — slower, larger in feeling, shaped by light and open sky. Planning one well is mostly a matter of sequence: do the right things in the right order, far enough ahead, and the day arrives calm. Here is the timeline we use.

## 10 to 12 months before: lock the date and the estate

Peak wedding season near Bhopal is winter, and the best dates go a year out. Start here:

- Confirm your **guest count** and rough function list (mehndi, sangeet, haldi, the wedding, reception, farewell brunch).
- Visit the estate and walk the [venues and lawns](/weddings) — picture each function in a real space.
- Block the date and reserve a [room allotment](/rooms) for your party.

The earlier you book, the more of the estate's [eleven event spaces](/venues) you can hold for your exclusive use.

## 6 to 9 months before: design the flow

This is where a forest wedding is won or lost. With a named estate planner, map:

- **Which function goes where** — an open lawn for the haldi, a [pillarless hall](/blog/why-the-aria-grand-is-pillarless) for the reception, the lakeside for cocktails.
- **A weather backup for every outdoor function** — the reason five of the eleven spaces are indoor halls.
- **Catering and menus** — much of it seasonal, from [Royalton Farms](/royalton) on the estate.

## 3 to 4 months before: the details

- Finalise décor, the mandap, lighting and the timeline for each day.
- Confirm vendor access, sound, and the movement of guests between functions.
- Add the experiences only this place offers — a [Ratapani safari](/blog/safaris-from-the-gate) for early-arriving guests, a [spa](/spa) morning for the wedding party.

## The final month: hand it over

By now the plan should be detailed enough that you can stop planning and start anticipating. Your planner runs the final walk-through, confirms room assignments, and holds the master timeline so you don't have to.

> The couples who enjoy their own wedding are the ones who finished planning a month early. The estate does the rest.

## A note on the season

A monsoon or shoulder-season wedding can be spectacular — green, dramatic, and quieter on the calendar — precisely because the [pillarless halls](/blog/why-the-aria-grand-is-pillarless) make weather a non-issue. Don't rule out the off-peak months; sometimes they give you the whole estate to yourselves.
