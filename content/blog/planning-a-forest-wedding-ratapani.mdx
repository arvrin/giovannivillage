A forest wedding runs on the same spreadsheet as any other Indian wedding — plus a handful of decisions that city hotels never force: which lawn for which light, what the weather call deadline is, and how to get forty of the groom's friends into safari jeeps by 6 a.m. the morning after the sangeet. Here is the timeline that makes it all boring — in the best way — by the week itself.

## 12 months out: lock the date, then the estate

Work backwards from the muhurat. The peak window — Dev Uthani Ekadashi (mid-November) through Basant Panchami (early February) — is when Bhopal's weather is perfect and when everyone else wants your weekend too; capacity venues genuinely book 9–12 months out. April–June muhurats buy you availability and better rates in exchange for moving day functions indoors.

Site visit checklist for any estate you're considering:

- Walk **every** venue at the hour you'd use it — a lawn at 4 p.m. and the same lawn at 7 p.m. are different rooms.
- Count real room inventory against your guest list, not the brochure's.
- Ask for the named planner you'd work with, not the sales manager.
- Ask what happens if it rains. If the answer takes more than one sentence, keep looking.

## 9 months out: sequence the functions

This is where an estate pays off — each function gets its own setting instead of the same ballroom re-dressed. A sequence that works beautifully across our grounds:

| Function | Venue | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mehndi (morning) | Gourmet Lake Side Lawn | Water light, shade, unhurried |
| Haldi | Sudesh II — lawn in the trees | Contained, colourful, close to rooms |
| Sangeet (night) | Aria Grand or Sudesh I | Stage, sound and a 25-ft ceiling — or 1,500 under the stars |
| Pheras (dawn or dusk) | Lawn ringed by forest | The photographs the forest was hired for |
| Reception | Aria Grand + attached lawn | Scales to 2,000 without a pillar in a single frame |
| Farewell brunch | The farm | Send them home fed from [Royalton](/blog/inside-royalton-farms) |

Assign every outdoor function its indoor twin *now*, in writing. The [pillarless Aria Grand](/blog/why-the-aria-grand-is-pillarless) partitions into three, so even three simultaneous contingencies have somewhere to go.

## 6 months out: rooms, travel, vendors

**Room block.** Map guests to the estate's ten room categories — elders near the venues, families in the master suites, the baraat crowd wherever the noise won't matter. Overflow lists go to the planner, not to guests.

**Travel grid.** Bhopal makes this easy — ~20 daily flights into Raja Bhoj (21 km from the estate), the Delhi Vande Bharat into Rani Kamlapati (10–12 km). Publish arrival guidance with the invitation; run airport shuttles in waves, not per-guest.

**Vendors.** Decorators, photographers and choreographers who've worked forest light before are worth their premium — dusk under a canopy is gorgeous and merciless in equal measure. The estate's panel has done these lawns dozens of times; outside vendors are welcome and get a venue walkthrough.

## 3 months out: the safari question, menus, weather doctrine

**Guest safaris.** The morning-after-sangeet safari has become the signature of weddings here — but permits at [Ratapani](/blog/ratapani-tiger-reserve-guide) have small quotas, so guest groups are booked through the travel desk now, not that week. (Marrying July–September? The reserve is closed for monsoon — swap in Bhimbetka's rock shelters, which never close.)

**Menus.** Tasting visit. Build the family recipes in with the kitchen — the brief is your menu, executed with the farm's produce, not a hotel's standard file with your names on it.

**Weather doctrine.** Agree the decision deadline for each outdoor function (typically 4–6 hours out), who makes the call — planner plus one named family member, nobody else — and what moves where. Decided now, a weather call in the week itself is logistics, not drama.

## The week itself

Guests land, shuttles run, the planner owns the clock. Your only actual job: eat breakfast every day. In our experience the couples who do are the ones who remember their own wedding.
