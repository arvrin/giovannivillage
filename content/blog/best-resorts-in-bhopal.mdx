Most "best resorts in Bhopal" lists are written by people who have never driven down Kolar Road, which is how a city this specific ends up described in interchangeable adjectives. This one is written from the estate itself. We'll be plain about our bias: we think the best resort in Bhopal is ours, and below are the reasons, specific enough for you to check.

First, a map. Bhopal's stays split into **three worlds**: heritage palace hotels in the old city's hills, branded business hotels in the commercial core, and the forest side on the southern edge, where [India's newest tiger reserve](/blog/ratapani-tiger-reserve-guide) begins. The first two are hotels. Only the third is a resort in the full sense: somewhere you arrive, park the car, and stay put.

## Giovanni Village: the luxury resort in Bhopal that is a whole estate

Giovanni Village sits on 23 acres at 410, Kolar Road, Kalapani, on the edge of Ratapani Tiger Reserve. Mango orchards with eight heirloom varieties, lily ponds, lakeside paths, and Royalton Farms, the working organic farm inside the gates that supplies most of what reaches the table.

**The rooms.** Ten categories, every one opening to green. They run from the King Room with Pool and Garden View, from ₹9,999 a night, through junior suites with private decks and gardens, to suites with open-to-sky baths and open-to-sky plunge pools. At the top is the Royal Suite: 1,100 sq ft inside, a 2,000 sq ft private garden, its own plunge pool and an in-room cinema, more than 3,000 sq ft of private space in all.

**The food.** Three restaurants, each with its own hour. Gourmet By The Woods is the fine-dining room under the canopy, cooking from the morning's harvest. Pihu is the rooftop, where [dinner comes with telescopes](/blog/stargazing-dinners-near-bhopal) once the sky darkens. Gazebo by the Lake is the quiet one, lantern-lit beside the water. Breakfast can be taken out at the farm itself.

**The slow part.** Elysium Spa does the long, unhurried treatments, with rituals shaped by the plant knowledge of the Gond, Ratapani's original keepers. [A day there](/blog/a-day-at-elysium-spa) is a reasonable use of a whole afternoon.

## The family resort near Bhopal built for kids

Travelling with children re-ranks everything. The view matters less than whether the grounds are safe to run on, and the tasting menu matters less than whether dinner can happen at six. This is the use the estate was largely built for.

There are **twenty on-site activities**, and children will find most of them before you do: the Junior Chef Academy, where the kitchen lets them cook under supervision; the soft-touch pool and its sunbathing lawn; cycling and kick-scooters on the estate paths; croquet, badminton and tennikoit, frisbee and Modern Kanche on the lawns; a board-games library for the hot hours; jhoolas under the trees; and lakeside fishing on the lily-pond deck, where whatever gets caught comes back at dinner. At **4:30 PM the cows are milked**, and city children meeting the source of their milk is reliably the highlight of the trip. After dark there are films at the open-air theatre, with love seats and fresh popcorn.

The adults' list overlaps: the Royalton Farms tour, a farm breakfast, the self-service barbecue, a visit to the Giovanni factory, the telescope dinner at Pihu, and the spa. The full set is on [our experiences page](/experiences).

The rooms are built for families too. The Master Suite and the Junior Suite with Plunge Pool sleep two adults and two children; the Royal Suite takes three adults and two children. Our [family resort guide](/blog/family-resorts-near-bhopal) has a two-day itinerary, hour by hour.

## The resort near Ratapani, for wildlife

Ratapani was notified as a tiger reserve in December 2024, which makes Bhopal the only state capital whose edge runs into one. Giovanni Village is the region's only luxury resort built on that edge, so you don't have to choose between the forest and a proper bed.

Safari permits are limited and must be booked ahead; the concierge [arranges them for you](/blog/safaris-from-the-gate). The usual rhythm is a morning drive and back for breakfast at the farm, with the rest of the day on the estate. Safaris pause for the monsoon, July to September, when the estate is at its greenest and the lily ponds are full. For the seasons, read [when to visit Ratapani](/blog/best-time-to-visit-ratapani). For a day off the estate, the [Bhimbetka rock shelters](/blog/bhimbetka-rock-shelters-guide) are close enough to fit into an afternoon.

## Weddings and events on one estate

Giovanni Village has **eleven venues, five indoor and six outdoor**, for gatherings from twenty guests to two thousand. The Aria Grand is a pillarless banquet hall with an attached lawn; the lakeside lawn beside Gourmet By The Woods, the Pihu Deck and the Cocktail Lawn take smaller functions. Catering comes from the resort's own kitchens, and guests sleep on site, so a three-day wedding never has to leave the gates. Our [wedding venues guide](/blog/destination-wedding-venues-near-bhopal) walks through each space.

## The rest of Bhopal's stays, briefly

Bhopal has good hotels that serve different trips. The **heritage palace hotels** in the old city's hills suit travellers for whom the Nawabi city, its lakes and museums are the destination. The **branded business hotels** in the commercial districts suit a work trip or a night between trains. Further out, a handful of simpler forest lodges and government-run camps serve travellers for whom the safari is the whole point and comfort comes second. Each fits its purpose; none is a resort estate in the sense above.
